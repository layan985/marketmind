# Negative Results & Failed Claims

This ledger exists so that failure is part of the public research record rather than an invisible editing decision.

## Entry rule

An entry is created when a frozen or materially documented test shows that a method is unstable, redundant, unsupported, dominated, non-reproducible, or outside its claimed operating range.

Each entry must record:

- ID and date;
- claim or assumption tested;
- specification and data version;
- expected behavior;
- observed behavior;
- uncertainty where relevant;
- interpretation;
- whether code, defaults, documentation, or theory changed;
- unresolved disagreement;
- reproducibility path.

## Current ledger

No formal negative-result entry has yet been frozen under this ledger.

This is **not** a statement that all MarketMind hypotheses are correct. It means the negative-results registry was introduced on 16 August 2026 and awaits results produced under the defined entry rule.

## Mandatory future entries

The following must be entered when observed rather than omitted from publication:

- a MarketMind benchmark losing to a simpler comparator;
- a metric becoming unstable below a documented sample size;
- transfer entropy failing under a specified confounding/noise regime;
- a window specification producing excessive state turnover;
- a preregistered prospective hypothesis not being supported;
- an independent reproduction failing or materially diverging;
- an external reviewer identifying an unresolved methodological defect.
