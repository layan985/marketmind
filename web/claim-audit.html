01 / THE CLAIM LADDER

Detection is not attribution.
Attribution is not identification.

C0 asks whether the observed process changed. C1 names an observable target such as volatility or covariance. C2 claims the conditional or temporal mechanism changed. C3 claims a direction, edge or intervention-relevant effect is identified.

The evidence burden increases at every step. A method can be excellent at C0 and scientifically unjustified at C2. A causal method can correctly stop at an equivalence class instead of inventing a C3 answer.

C0 / CHANGE

Something changed.

Null calibration, break-magnitude power, delay/localization and sample stability.

C1 / STATE

A named observable changed.

Direct target recovery, alternative observable explanations and target-appropriate robustness.

C2 / MECHANISM

The machine changed.

Silent mechanism worlds, nuisance inversion, proxy collapse, metamorphic invariance and difficulty frontiers.

C3 / IDENTIFICATION

A structural direction is identified.

Observational equivalence, explicit assumptions, abstention and identifying evidence.

02 / RCA-1 — THE BENCHMARK CAUGHT US

A good AUC was hiding a bad invariance property.

The raw VAR coefficient-distance baseline still detects the silent mechanism shift at 0.804 AUC. But when each variable is multiplied by a different fixed positive constant — preserving the existence and timing of the target switch — its score ordering changes severely.

That means the detector had real power while its claimed structural distance remained contaminated by arbitrary coordinate scale.

03 / METAMORPHIC VALIDATION

Ground truth is rare.
Invariances are testable.

RCA uses target-preserving transformations when real-world ground truth is unavailable. If a transformation is known not to change the scientific target but materially changes the model conclusion, the claim is representation-dependent.

Examples can include unit rescaling, variable permutation, timestamp translation, target-preserving nuisance injection or other transformations declared in advance for the exact estimand.

04 / GRAND CHALLENGE 001

Stop asking who wins one metric.

The open challenge asks what claim level different methods actually earn. Candidate methods include PCMCI+, LPCMCI, VAR-LiNGAM, SPACETIME-style approaches, HMM/change-point models, transfer entropy and deliberately simple baselines.

Unknown cells stay unknown until somebody runs them. A sophisticated method is allowed to fail. A simple method is allowed to win. A non-identifiable case is allowed to end in abstention.
