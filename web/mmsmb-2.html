01 / THE FALSIFICATION

Same covariance.
Different propagation.

For each regime, x(t) = A(r)x(t−1) + ε(t). A different innovation covariance is chosen so both regimes share the same stationary covariance: Q(r) = Σ − A(r)ΣA(r)ᵀ. The measured pre/post stationary-covariance difference is 2.7×10−15, numerical zero.

04 / THE THIRD ANSWER

Sometimes the answer is
you cannot know.

A two-variable linear-Gaussian track constructs X→Y and Y→X models that produce the same observational Gaussian law. Across 800 generated datasets, a classifier using sample moments reaches 0.516 mean 10-fold CV accuracy—essentially chance. Yet interventions imply different effects.

The desirable output in such a case is an equivalence class, an abstention, or the additional assumption required for identification—not confident guessing.
