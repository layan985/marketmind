Realized volatility beats MII on MMSMB-1.

Using a frozen 900-session development half and held-out synthetic test half, realized volatility recovers 71.5% of latent regime labels. MII recovers 48.3%.

This is retained as a benchmark loss. MMSMB-1 makes volatility, connectivity and memory co-move; in that environment, additional MII machinery does not earn its complexity.
