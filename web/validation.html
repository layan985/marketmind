01 / ADOPTION SCORECARD

External evidence, or it does not count.

The target window is 9 August 2026 through 9 February 2027. Stars, downloads, private praise, self-authored issues, and unverifiable testimonials are excluded.

A counted user must leave an inspectable public record: issue, reproduction report, notebook, repository, substantive PR, research object, or equivalent durable evidence.

01

5 independent uses

Five distinct non-author users with a named public person, lab, course, project, or repository and a verifiable MarketMind use record.

02

Genuine issues opened by accounts other than the primary author. Author-created tracking issues never count.

03

1 outside PR

A substantive non-author contribution that is reviewed on scientific and software quality and merged.

04

Fresh environment, exact version/commit, commands, outputs, discrepancies and outcome recorded publicly.

Public event record plus durable slides, notebook, recording or equivalent research artifact.

A public notebook, paper, preprint, teaching object or empirical workflow that actually uses MarketMind.
