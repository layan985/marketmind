Common Driver Trap

150 RUNS

Question. Can pairwise directional information invent B→C when B and C share a lagged driver A?

Known DAG. A→B and A→C; no B→C path.

DGP. AR(1) A, B and C; common-driver strength s ∈ {0,.3,.6,.9,1.2}. Independent Gaussian innovations.

Estimator. Three-bin plug-in conditional mutual information. Naive TE = I(Bₜ₋₁;Cₜ|Cₜ₋₁). Conditioned TE adds Aₜ₋₁. n=4,000; 30 replications per strength; seeds 100–129.
