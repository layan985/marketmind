LAB-004

One Outlier, Different Market

RUN / ROBUSTNESS GAP

Inject one extreme return into one asset and re-estimate average cross-asset dependence across 100 replications. In this design, the mean absolute change in the Pearson statistic is 0.00436 versus 0.000356 for Spearman.

The resulting sensitivity ratio is 12.26×. This is a prompt to report robust dependence alternatives, not a claim that rank correlation is universally superior.
