01 / HIDDEN TRUTH

A benchmark should know the answer.

Regime connectivity takes values .20, .50, .82; volatility scales are .006, .010, .017; memory parameters are .05, .25, .48. At synthetic session 900 the directed graph changes.

Before the break: A01→A02 and A03→A04. After the break: A05→A06 and A07→A09. The panel also contains two disclosed outliers, sparse missingness and one contiguous A08 missing block.

02 / SIMPLE BASELINE

Start with correlation, not sophistication.

A 60-session average absolute-correlation baseline reaches 0.912 AUC for the disclosed high-connectivity state. Its correlation with latent connectivity is 0.644.

That baseline is deliberately difficult to dismiss: a complex model should demonstrate what it adds beyond it.

04 / CHALLENGE
