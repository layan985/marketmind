01 / RULES

Known answer. Frozen test.

Use the first 900 sessions for development and the final 900 for evaluation. If labels need permutation, learn the mapping on development data only. Do not use held-out labels for tuning, mapping or model selection. Report held-out accuracy, adjusted Rand index and normalized mutual information, plus code, version, preprocessing and seeds.
