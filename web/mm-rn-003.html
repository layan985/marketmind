How Much Alpha Can
Look-Ahead Bias Manufacture?

A backtest on IID Gaussian noise produced mean annualized Sharpe 21.017 when the signal saw the same-session return it was credited with. A centered rolling window produced 2.796. The causal control produced 0.019.

01 / QUESTION

What happens when the information boundary is wrong?

Each replication draws 4,000 IID N(0, 0.01) returns. There is no genuine predictive structure to discover. Any persistent performance must therefore come from sampling variation, retrospective selection or contamination introduced by the backtest design.

02 / FIVE ARMS
01

Causal trailing

20-session rolling mean shifted one session. Decision t uses returns through t-1.

02

Centered window

21-session centered rolling mean. Future observations enter the value attached to t.

03

Same-session

Signal sign(r_t) receives r_t. Deliberately pathological.

04

Threshold search

Best of 17 cutoffs chosen after the realized noise is observed.

05

Best of 50 noise

Fifty unrelated signals are searched and the strongest backtest is retained.

05 / LIMITS

This is a contamination experiment, not a return claim.

The study does not imply that a real strategy with Sharpe 2.8 contains this bug, nor that every timing error inflates performance. IID Gaussian returns omit serial dependence, volatility clustering, jumps, heavy tails, cross-sectional dependence and market microstructure. The same-session arm is intentionally unrealistic. Transaction costs are omitted because the estimand is contamination magnitude. The prospective MarketMind holdout is untouched.
