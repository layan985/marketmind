MM-BENCH-009 · MMSMB-2 · 200 REPLICATIONS01

Same covariance. Different market machine.

We changed the VAR propagation mechanism while holding stationary contemporaneous covariance fixed. Mean volatility, average correlation and PC1 concentration stayed at chance: 0.505, 0.497 and 0.497 AUC. A simple VAR-coefficient shift reached 0.797 AUC.

Controlled synthetic evidence; the VAR score is a change detector, not a causal-identification guarantee.
