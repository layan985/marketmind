LAB-002 · SYNTHETIC · 100 RUNS01

How much alpha can a bug manufacture?

On IID noise, the causal trailing control averaged 0.02 annualized Sharpe. A centered rolling window averaged 2.80. Same-session execution averaged 21.02.

LAB-001 · COMMON DRIVER TRAP02

Directionality can appear where no direct path exists.

As common-driver strength rose from 0 to 1.2, naive B→C transfer entropy rose from 0.0014 to 0.0175 nats. Conditioning on the driver cut the high-strength estimate by about 59%.

Finite-sample estimator bias remains visible; this is an operating-envelope warning, not a universal correction guarantee.

LAB-003 · NETWORK AMPUTATION03

A market state can be more hub-sensitive than it looks.

Removing the dominant synthetic asset flipped 7.2% of fixed-threshold state labels. Removing a random asset flipped 1.9%, even though the metric series remained highly correlated.
