01 / METHOD

One index. Three different descriptions of market structure.

The Market Intelligence Index (MII) combines three dimensions. Memory measures persistence and fractal structure. Information flow measures entropy and dependence. Connectivity measures how markets organize as a network.

The default MII weights are fixed at 0.35 memory, 0.40 information flow, and 0.25 connectivity. Regimes are learned causally from trailing history rather than from future observations.
