MM-BENCH-001 · MMSMB-1 · HELD-OUT01

Realized volatility beat MII at recovering the latent regime.

After development-sample state alignment, realized volatility recovered 71.5% of held-out synthetic regime labels. MII recovered 48.3%.

This is a MarketMind benchmark loss. It is synthetic measurement evidence, not a real-market trading comparison.

MM-BENCH-003 · REDUNDANCY02

Average correlation reproduced 95.3% of connectivity-state assignments.

On held-out MMSMB-1, average absolute correlation correlates 0.981 with the connectivity component and reproduces 95.3% of fixed-tercile states. MST coherence reproduces 100% in this design.

The connectivity composite does not earn its extra complexity on this benchmark alone.

LAB-001 · COMMON DRIVER04

Directionality can appear where no direct path exists.

At common-driver strength 1.2, naive B→C transfer entropy is 0.01754 nats. Conditioning on the known driver reduces it to 0.00725, a 58.7% reduction.

At weak confounding the finite-sample conditional estimator has a positive floor.
