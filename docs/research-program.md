# Research program

MarketMind Research is organized around four programs: Market Memory & Persistence, Information Flow, Market Networks, and Regime Measurement & Validation.

The object under evaluation is not complexity for its own sake. Every component must face known-answer tests, perturbations, sensitivity analysis, and appropriate simpler comparators.

See the repository-level [research program](https://github.com/layan985/marketmind/blob/main/RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md) for scopes, publication IDs, and interpretation rules.
