# Governance and research integrity

MarketMind separates six evidence states that are often conflated in quantitative software: implementation tests, retrospective empirical results, prospective confirmation, independent reproduction, external methodological review, and institutional/client work.

The prospective holdout is sealed through 6 August 2027. Public counters for replications, reviews, applications, publications, datasets, studies, releases, and benchmarks resolve to the machine-readable `registry/` files.

Negative or failed tests are publishable research objects. Deviations are time-stamped. Public proxy data are not described as exact reproductions of licensed histories.
