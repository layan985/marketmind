# Benchmarks

MarketMind uses frozen comparative specifications rather than retrospective winner selection.

The initial benchmark program covers multiscale regime discrimination, leakage resistance, network-metric redundancy, and directional-information validation. Full specifications are in the repository `benchmarks/` directory and status is machine-readable in `registry/benchmarks.json`.

A benchmark does not become evidence until its data/configuration/code and complete result artifact are frozen.
