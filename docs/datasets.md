# Datasets

MarketMind distinguishes data sources from research results. Dataset records describe what entered an analysis, under what license/proxy status, at what vintage, and with what transformations.

## Dataset release fields

`dataset_id` · `version` · `source` · `evidence_class` · `coverage` · `retrieval_date` · `vintage` · `license` · `schema` · `transformations` · `quality_checks` · `hash` · `limitations` · `citation`

## Current policy

Public proxies are labeled as public proxies. Licensed-vendor equivalence is never implied. Synthetic data remains synthetic even when it is designed to mimic realistic market structure.

See [Data and reproducibility](data.md) for the pipeline contract and reproducibility requirements.
