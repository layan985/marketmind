# Institutional research

MarketMind is public research infrastructure. The commercial product is **independent research-integrity work**, not software licenses, trade signals, or performance promises.

## Quant Research Integrity Audit

**Founding scope: US$2,000–5,000**

A bounded audit for quant research teams, investment research groups, fintech research functions, academic labs, and technical due-diligence teams that need to know whether an apparent result survives contact with timing, leakage, costs, validation design and reproducibility.

The audit inspects:

- data timestamp and feature-availability contracts;
- target and label construction;
- train / validation / test separation;
- scaler, threshold and regime timing;
- look-ahead and survivorship channels;
- parameter selection and multiple testing;
- transaction costs, turnover and execution assumptions;
- benchmark choice and holdout contamination;
- robustness and sensitivity;
- environment capture, deterministic rerun and artifact integrity.

### Deliverables

1. **Leakage map** — every possible information channel classified PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED.
2. **Timing audit** — when each input becomes knowable and when each position or prediction can legally act.
3. **Rerun record** — fresh-environment reproduction where the supplied artifacts permit it.
4. **Robustness battery** — pre-specified perturbations, costs, alternative baselines and sensitivity checks relevant to the research claim.
5. **Failure register** — discrepancies, severity, evidence, affected claims and remediation state.
6. **Decision memo** — what can be defended now, what requires another test, and what should not be claimed.
7. **Reproducibility package** — commands, environment, hashes and permission-safe evidence record.

The audit does not certify a strategy as profitable and does not convert an observational backtest into a causal claim.
