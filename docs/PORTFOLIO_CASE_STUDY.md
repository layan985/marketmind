# Portfolio case study — MarketMind MII

> From award-winning financial-market research to falsifiable, DOI-backed software.

[Portfolio](https://layan-research-portfolio.r8ms5bfzb6.chatgpt.site) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/marketmind/) · [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21844956) · [OSF registration](https://osf.io/nyseh/overview)

## The research problem

Technical signals often appear to work in one market state and fail in another. *The Emergent Market Mind* proposes a structural explanation: markets behave as changing information networks whose memory, information flow and connectivity can be measured jointly.

The paper received the **2026 Charles H. Dow Award** from the CMT Association. MarketMind turns its Market Intelligence Index (MII) into inspectable software rather than leaving the method inside a manuscript.

## What is original

- A three-part MII combining market memory, information flow and network connectivity.
- Causal expanding or development-frozen normalization rather than full-sample scaling.
- Rolling regime thresholds learned only from information available at the evaluation date.
- A fixed regime-to-signal hypothesis tested with explicit transaction costs, execution delay and naive baselines.

## Claim boundary

Historical regime results generated the hypothesis. They are **not** presented as prospective confirmation. The confirmatory result will be reported after the frozen holdout regardless of whether MarketMind succeeds, fails or produces mixed evidence.
