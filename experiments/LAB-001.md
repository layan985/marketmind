# LAB-001 — Common Driver Trap

**Status:** COMPLETE · RESULT AVAILABLE  
**Generated:** 17 August 2026  
**Ground truth:** A → B and A → C; no B → C edge.

## Question
Can pairwise directional-information estimation manufacture an apparent B → C relationship when B and C share a lagged driver, and how much does conditioning on A remove?

## DGP
A_t = 0.55 A_{t-1}+ε_A; B_t = 0.30 B_{t-1}+sA_{t-1}+ε_B; C_t = 0.30 C_{t-1}+sA_{t-1}+ε_C, with independent Gaussian innovations. Driver strength s ∈ {0,0.3,0.6,0.9,1.2}.

## Estimator and run grid
Three-quantile discretization. Naive TE estimates I(B_{t-1};C_t|C_{t-1}); conditioned estimate I(B_{t-1};C_t|C_{t-1},A_{t-1}). n=4,000 per run. 30 replications at each of five strengths = **150 runs**. Seeds 100–129 are reused across strength cells.

## Results
| s | Naive TE mean | Conditioned TE mean |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0 | .001445 | .004613 |
| 0.3 | .002958 | .004661 |
| 0.6 | .009654 | .005271 |
| 0.9 | .015106 | .006183 |
| 1.2 | .017541 | .007253 |

At s=1.2, conditioning reduces the mean estimate by **58.7%**.

## Failure boundary
At weak driver strength, the finite-sample plug-in conditional estimator has a visible positive floor. “Conditioning fixes confounding” would therefore be an overclaim. This experiment does not establish that all transfer-entropy estimators behave identically.
