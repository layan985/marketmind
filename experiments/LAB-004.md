# LAB-004 — One Outlier, Different Market

**Status:** COMPLETE · RESULT AVAILABLE

## Question
How sensitive is a simple cross-asset dependence summary to a single extreme observation under Pearson versus rank correlation?

## DGP
Eight assets share a Gaussian common factor plus independent noise. One +15-unit outlier is injected into one asset at the midpoint. n=500 per run; 100 replications; seeds 200–299.

## Result
Mean absolute change in average Pearson dependence = **0.004363**; Spearman = **0.000356**. Ratio of means = **12.26×**. Median changes are 0.003879 and 0.000209 respectively.

## Interpretation and boundary
This is evidence that one conventional dependence summary can be much more outlier-sensitive in this design. It is **not** a universal claim that Spearman is superior; rank methods trade away other information and can fail under different structures.
