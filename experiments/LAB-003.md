# LAB-003 — Network Amputation

**Status:** COMPLETE · RESULT AVAILABLE

## Question
Can an aggregate connectivity series appear almost unchanged after an important node is removed while discrete market-state assignments still change materially?

## Ground truth and DGP
A seven-asset synthetic panel alternates between lower- and higher-coherence latent regimes. Asset 1 is designed as the dominant factor-loading hub. n=2,200; 80-session trailing windows; seed 42.

## Result
Full-panel latent-regime AUC = **0.8645**. Removing the hub reduces AUC to **0.8502**; removing a random asset yields **0.8609**. The full and hub-removed connectivity series correlate **0.9951**, yet **7.21%** of fixed-threshold state labels change. Random removal changes **1.93%**.

## Interpretation
A high correlation between two continuous metric series is not evidence of classification invariance. Universe robustness must be tested at the output actually consumed by downstream research.

## Failure boundary
This is a deliberately hub-dominated synthetic network. It motivates MM-BENCH-006; it does not establish real-market universe sensitivity.
