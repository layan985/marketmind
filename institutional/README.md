# Institutional Research

Paid work is kept separate from scientific validation. A client engagement cannot create a public validation badge merely by purchasing work.

## Quant Research Integrity Audit

Input: a quantitative strategy or research pipeline with enough code/data/specification to inspect.

Review domains:

- data timing and source provenance;
- feature availability;
- normalization and scaling;
- label/regime construction;
- execution timing;
- transaction-cost assumptions;
- parameter selection;
- cross-validation and holdout design;
- multiple testing;
- survivorship and universe construction;
- reproducibility and artifact integrity.

Deliverables: leakage map, independent rerun where feasible, failure map, robustness battery, issue register, and reproducibility package.

## Market Structure Study

Input: a defined market universe or client-authorized dataset.

Outputs: memory analysis, information-flow map, network structure, regime measurement, uncertainty, sensitivity, comparison with conventional models, provenance, and a reproducible package.

## Boundary

Neither scope promises trading profits, alpha, investment suitability, or a favorable finding. The work product is an inspectable research result.
