## Submission rule

Do not submit on a calendar trigger alone. Reassess only when the repository shows sustained public development, iterative releases, research use, and evidence that another researcher can install, test, and interrogate the software.

## Statement-of-need preparation

A future software paper should compare MarketMind honestly with commonly used alternatives: regime-switching/statistical-state tooling, HMM tooling, network-analysis libraries, entropy/information packages, and general quantitative research libraries.

The defensible need is an **integrated, leakage-aware, provenance-producing regime-research workflow**, not a claim that MarketMind invented each component method.
