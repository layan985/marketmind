# MarketMind 0.1.1 — Preregistered Validation Infrastructure

This release links MarketMind to the prospective out-of-sample validation of the Market Intelligence Index and strengthens the project's reproducibility and open-science infrastructure.

## Added

- Public OSF preregistration for the 2026–2027 prospective validation.
- Associated OSF research project.
- Frozen prospective validation configuration.
- Field-by-field preregistration documentation.
- Machine-readable deviation log.
- Dedicated preregistration freeze branch.
- ORCID-linked citation and archival metadata.
- Expanded Zenodo metadata for persistent software archiving.

## Prospective validation

The confirmatory holdout runs from **10 August 2026 through 6 August 2027**.

The preregistered regime mapping is:

- High MII → trend-following.
- Medium MII → breakout / volatility expansion.
- Low MII → mean reversion.

OSF preregistration: https://osf.io/nyseh/overview

Associated OSF project: https://osf.io/649gj

## Reproducibility

The preregistration freezes the MarketMind implementation, primary markets, technical-signal definitions, execution timing, transaction costs, statistical inference procedures, exclusions, robustness checks, and null-results commitment before the prospective holdout begins.
