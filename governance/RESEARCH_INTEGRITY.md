# Research Integrity Policy

1. Separate controlled implementation evidence, retrospective empirical results, prospective confirmation, external review, independent reproduction, and client data.
2. Never convert a missing validation record into an implied endorsement.
3. Preserve null, adverse, and contradictory results when generated under a frozen study.
4. Time-stamp material deviations and identify whether they occurred before or after outcome observation.
5. Preserve code/data/configuration sufficient to inspect material quantitative claims where licensing permits.
6. Distinguish exact reproduction from proxy-data reimplementation.
7. Correct public claims when the underlying evidence changes.
